MyMoney

MyMoney is a free personal finance application that helps you track income and expenses locally on your device. Your financial records are stored on your device and are not sent to our servers.

The app optionally uses the Google Drive API (scope drive.appdata) to back up and restore your database in the application-specific hidden folder (appDataFolder) of your own Google Drive. MyMoney cannot view, modify, or delete any other files in your Google Drive.

MyMoney does not use Google APIs for AI-generated Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (AI NCII).
